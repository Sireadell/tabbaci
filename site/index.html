What the courtroom does with your sentence



  
It fetches both pages inside a sealed chip, so nobody can swap the evidence. Three AI models, each signing its own answer, are shown your sentence in a fixed frame labelled as untrusted data. If two or more say it is true and it is not what the pages say, that is a lie that got through, and it goes on the ledger with the page it contradicted.

  
Today's judges are test stand-ins on a test network, and the page says so on every ruling. Real model judges are switched in when their access is paid for.
