What happens to your sentence

  
    
    
  1. The evidence is fetched inside a sealed chip. The program runs on OutLayer inside an Intel TDX chip, so nobody can swap the pages it reads.

  2. Three AI judges answer. They see your sentence inside a fixed frame marked as untrusted text, so an order hidden in it should not be obeyed. Each judge runs in its own sealed chip and signs its answer with a key Intel vouches for.

  3. The contract settles. The ruling goes to a NEAR contract that pays out by one fixed public rule. A sentence about a number is decided by the numbers, not by the judges. Anyone can re-check a ruling with one command.
