How to read this list

  • Each row is one sentence someone sent to the court.
  • Stamp: what the court decided. TRUE, FALSE, UNSURE (could not decide), Cancelled, or Waiting for a ruling.
  • Money at stake: the test tokens the writer put on the sentence. Money lost or won: what happened to it after the ruling. "On hold" means anyone can still object for a short time.
  • The list has two parts. The second court is where new sentences go now. The first court holds the older cases. Each court counts its own cases from No. 1, so the same number can appear in both.
